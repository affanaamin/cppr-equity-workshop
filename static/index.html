01 · THE EMPIRICAL REALITY

Approved. Budgeted. Yet unequal.

Why an equity audit across the full budget cycle matters in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

KP EDUCATION DIVIDE · FY 2025–26
4.9M
Out-of-School Children Ages 5–16

59% of out-of-school children are girls and 53% of dropouts are female. Out of KP's Rs. 363B education budget (17% of Rs. 2,119B total), most goes to recurring salaries — leaving only Rs. 5B for literacy and dropout reduction.

Indicators Across the Budget Cycle

Approved does not mean delivered. In Phase I, the Disability Rehabilitation Fund remained unspent and textbook delivery fell to 25–30%. Both lines passed planning and approval; neither passed an equity check.

LOCAL GOVERNANCE · ARTICLES 25, 25A & 34–38
Rs. 52.8B
2026–27 Local Gov Development ADP

Up from Rs. 12.0B (2005–15) and Rs. 291.5B (2019–26). Under the KP Local Government Act 2013 (Sections 25, 34 & 36), councils must disaggregate KPIs, publish bi-annual execution reports (31 Dec & 31 Jul), and hold public audit hearings.

BEYOND GENDER ALONE · INTERSECTIONAL LENS
7 + 1
Core Dimensions + Geographic Overlay

Every budget stage is audited across: 1. Gender · 2. Disability · 3. Refugee/Displacement · 4. Ethnicity · 5. Religious Minority · 6. Low-Income · 7. Age — plus Geographic Vulnerability (Newly Merged Districts & remote tehsils) as a cross-cutting lens.

03 · THE 6-STEP METHOD IN ACTION

From blind allocation to genuine equity.

Illustrative Audit · Budget Line Item 402 across the 6-Step Equity Method.

🔴 INITIAL LINE ITEM 402 · RED RATING
“Construction of 5 new community halls in underserved village and neighbourhood councils.”

Audit Finding: ✓ Geographic Vulnerability present (underserved councils). ✕ Silent on all 7 demographic dimensions (Gender, Disability, Refugee, Ethnicity, Religion, Low-Income, Age). Physical building is funded, but equitable access is ignored.

🟢 6-STEP EQUITY REWRITE · GREEN RATING
“Construction of 5 fully accessible community halls in underserved village and neighbourhood councils, with ramps and accessible toilets, women’s use times, open access for all ethnic and religious communities and displaced households, subsidised use for low-income households, and facilities for all ages.”

Equity Standard: ✓ Explicit inclusion across all 7 dimensions + geographic targeting + measurable delivery standards.

🟢 YES (Green)Explicit objective + disaggregated data + funded & monitored to outcome.
🟡 PARTIAL (Yellow)Acknowledged in general terms but lacks disaggregation or outcome tracking.
🔴 NO (Red)Absent — no explicit objective, disaggregated data, consultation, or monitoring.
